I served as a Teaching Assistant for UGOD 6102 Seminar at HKUST (Guangzhou), instructed by Prof. Muzhi Zhou. The course explored the practical use of artificial intelligence across academic and professional settings, including job interviews, research videos, data visualization, slide design, and job applications. It also featured invited early-career scholars who shared their experiences of navigating the academic job market and securing academic positions.
