<h2>Daily transitions and time pressure.</h2>
<p>Using UK time-use diaries from 2020–2021, this study examines how movement between activity domains relates to subjective time pressure across gender and workplace arrangements.</p>
<div class="rs-finding"><span class="rs-eyebrow">FINDINGS</span><p>For men, time pressure follows a U-shaped pattern across switching levels. For women, more switching is associated with greater pressure, with a weaker association among remote workers than onsite workers.</p></div>
